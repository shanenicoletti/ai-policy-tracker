## European Union

### [European Commission Consults on AI Act Fundamental Rights Impact Assessment Template](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/news/commission-seeks-feedback-draft-template-fundamental-rights-impact-assessment-under-ai-act)
*Medium importance · 2026-11-16 · Financial services*

**What happened:** The European Commission and the AI Office launched a public consultation and pilot testing program for a draft questionnaire template and explanatory notice under Article 27 of the AI Act. The template guides deployers of high-risk AI systems in evaluating impacts on fundamental rights before deployment.

**Why it matters:** The guidance directly affects public bodies and private deployers of high-risk AI, including financial institutions deploying AI for credit scoring and life or health insurance risk assessments. Covered deployers must complete and submit these assessments to market surveillance authorities prior to deployment ahead of the December 2027 enforcement deadline.

### [ECB Highlights AI Debt Financing and Cyber Threats as Key Financial Stability Risks](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/key/date/2026/html/ecb.sp261116~db374b56ab.ga.html)
*Medium importance · 2026-11-16 · Financial services*

**What happened:** In an address previewing the Financial Stability Review, ECB Vice-President Boris Vujcic identified AI as a top systemic risk to the euro area financial system, citing massive corporate debt issuance across the AI supply chain, market concentration, and rapid advances in AI cyber capabilities. The ECB also published an early-release special feature analyzing the cyber-AI nexus.

**Why it matters:** Euro area banks and financial intermediaries face heightened supervisory expectations from ECB Banking Supervision to adapt risk management frameworks, strengthen cyber defences, and manage critical dependencies on a concentrated set of cloud and AI service providers.
